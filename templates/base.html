Frequently Asked Questions

Can you mount a TV on a stone or brick fireplace without damaging the masonry?

Yes. We use specialized masonry anchors and diamond-tipped carbide bits to secure the mount directly into mortar joints or brick without cracking or damaging your fireplace structure.

How do you hide wires if my wall isn’t pre-wired?

We offer two solutions: in-wall cable routing (running wires inside the drywall for a completely invisible look) or sleek, paintable surface raceways/covers to keep cables organized and hidden.

Which areas of Southern Utah do you service?

We service St. George, Washington, Ivins, Santa Clara, Hurricane, La Verkin, and surrounding Southern Utah communities.

Can you mount 85"+ TVs or mount onto metal studs?

Absolutely. We handle large-format displays (up to 98"+) and carry specialized heavy-duty toggles designed specifically for metal stud commercial or condo installations.

Can you mount a TV in outdoor patio or pool areas?

Yes, we install outdoor-rated mounts on stucco, brick, tile, or wood siding using weather-resistant hardware.

Do you mount soundbars and hide streaming box cables?

Yes! We can mount soundbars directly below the TV and conceal cables for Apple TVs, Roku devices, cable boxes, and gaming consoles.

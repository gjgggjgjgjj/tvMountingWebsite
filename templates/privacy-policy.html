At Southern Utah TV Mounting, we are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights as a customer.

By providing your phone number and opting in, you may receive text messages from Southern Utah TV Mounting, including:

  • Appointment reminders and scheduling confirmations

Message frequency will vary based on your activity with us. Standard message and data rates may apply.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of personal information:

  • Name and contact details (phone number, email address, mailing address)
  • Service history and job records

We collect this information when you contact us, book a service, or interact with our communications.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your personal information to:

  • Schedule, confirm, and follow up on services
  • Send invoices and process payments
  • Provide customer support
  • Send you relevant offers and updates (with your consent)

We will not use your information for purposes unrelated to our services without your consent.

4. How We Store and Protect Your Data

We take the security of your information seriously. Your data is stored in secure, industry-standard systems with restricted access limited to authorized personnel only. We use reasonable safeguards to protect your information from unauthorized access, disclosure, or misuse.

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share your information with trusted third-party service providers who assist us in operating our business, such as:

  • Payment processors

These partners are required to handle your information securely and only for the purposes we specify. We may also disclose information if required by law.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal information we hold about you

  • Request that we delete your information, subject to any legal obligations

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us at samuel.teuscher@southernutahtvmounting.com or (928)533-5549.

To unsubscribe from text messages at any time, reply STOP to any message you receive from us. You will receive a confirmation and no further messages will be sent.

To get help, reply HELP to any of our messages, or contact us directly:
